# Yojanta — User Manual

A step-by-step guide for using the Yojanta platform.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1 — Register an Account
1. Open Yojanta in your browser
2. Click **"Register"** on the home page
3. Fill in your username, email, and password
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You will be automatically logged in

### Step 2 — Complete Your Profile
Your profile is what Yojanta uses to find schemes for you. The more complete it is, the better your matches will be.

1. Go to **Profile** from the navigation bar
2. Fill in all fields:

| Field | What to enter | Why it matters |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Full Name | Your legal name | Display purposes |
| Date of Birth | DD/MM/YYYY | Used to calculate your age for age-based eligibility |
| Gender | Male / Female / Other | Many schemes are gender-specific |
| Annual Income | Your yearly household income in ₹ | Income-based eligibility filter |
| Occupation | Student / Farmer / Government Employee / etc. | Some schemes target specific occupations |
| State | Your home state | State-specific scheme matching |
| Category | General / OBC / SC / ST | Reservation-based scheme eligibility |

3. Click **"Save Profile"**

---

## Using the Dashboard

After saving your profile, go to **Dashboard**.

You will see a list of government schemes you are **currently eligible for** based on your profile. Each card shows:
- Scheme name
- Department / Ministry
- Application deadline
- Documents required

### Filtering Schemes
Use the filter bar to narrow results:
- **Category** — Education, Agriculture, Health, Housing, etc.
- **Deadline** — show only schemes closing soon
- **Search** — type any keyword to search scheme names

---

## Opting Into a Scheme

When you find a scheme you want to apply for:
1. Click on the scheme card
2. Click **"Opt In"**
3. The scheme is added to your **Application Tracker**

---

## Application Tracker

Go to **Tracker** from the navigation bar to see all schemes you have opted into.

Update your status as you progress:

| Status | When to use |
|--------|------------|
| Opted | You have decided to apply but haven't submitted yet |
| Applied | You have submitted the application on the official portal |
| Approved | Your application was approved |
| Rejected | Your application was not approved |

To update: click the status dropdown next to the scheme → select the new status.

---

## Exploring All Schemes

Go to **Schemes** from the navigation bar to browse every scheme in the database — not just the ones you are eligible for.

Use this to:
- Discover schemes you may become eligible for in the future
- Check scheme details and required documents in advance
- Search by keyword, category, or department

---

## Notifications

Yojanta will alert you when:
- A scheme you are eligible for has a deadline within 7 days
- New schemes are added that match your profile

Check the notification bell icon in the navigation bar regularly.

---

## Updating Your Profile

If your circumstances change (income, state, category), update your profile:
1. Go to **Profile**
2. Update the relevant fields
3. Click **"Save Profile"**
4. Your Dashboard will automatically refresh with updated eligible schemes

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I filled my profile but the Dashboard shows no schemes. Why?**
A: Check that all profile fields are filled in completely — especially income, category, and date of birth. Also check that scheme deadlines haven't passed.

**Q: How often is scheme data updated?**
A: The database syncs with data.gov.in every day at midnight automatically.

**Q: Can I apply for a scheme directly through Yojanta?**
A: Yojanta helps you discover and track schemes. The actual application is submitted on the official government portal. Each scheme card shows where to apply.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Password reset functionality is coming in the next version. For now, please register a new account.

**Q: Is my personal data safe?**
A: Yes. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt. Your profile data is stored securely in MongoDB and is never shared.
